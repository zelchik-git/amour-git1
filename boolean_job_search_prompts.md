# 🔍 Master Job Search Queries & Studio Directory

This document contains pre-formulated **Boolean Search Strings**, **Google X-Ray ATS Queries**, **AI Search Prompts**, and a curated **Studio Careers Directory** targeting **Canada & Global Remote Concept Art Roles**.

---

## 1. 🌐 Master Boolean Search Strings

### 📌 Universal Concept Art Title Cluster
Use this title cluster across any search engine:
```text
("Concept Artist" OR "2D Artist" OR "Illustrator" OR "Digital Artist" OR "Concept Prop Artist" OR "Concept Art" OR "2D Concept Art" OR "Character Concept Artist" OR "Creature Concept Artist" OR "Visual Development Artist" OR "VisDev Artist")
```

---

### 🍁 A. Canada Targeted Search (LinkedIn / Indeed / Google)
Finds active Canadian positions (Montreal, Vancouver, Toronto, Hybrid, or Canada Remote):
```text
("Concept Artist" OR "2D Artist" OR "Illustrator" OR "Digital Artist" OR "Concept Prop Artist" OR "Character Concept Artist" OR "Visual Development") AND ("Canada" OR "Montreal" OR "Vancouver" OR "Toronto" OR "Quebec" OR "Edmonton") AND ("Game" OR "Gaming" OR "Animation" OR "Studio") -senior -lead -director
```
*(Tip: Remove `-senior -lead -director` if you want to include all levels).*

---

### 🌍 B. Worldwide Remote & Contract Search
Finds worldwide remote, freelance, or B2B contracts:
```text
("Concept Artist" OR "2D Artist" OR "Character Concept" OR "Prop Concept" OR "Illustrator") AND ("Remote" OR "Work from Home" OR "Worldwide" OR "Anywhere" OR "Contract" OR "Freelance") AND ("Game" OR "Entertainment" OR "VFX" OR "Studio")
```

---

### 🎯 C. Character & Creature Focused Search
Prioritizes your core specialization:
```text
("Character Concept Artist" OR "Creature Designer" OR "Character Artist 2D" OR "Costume Concept" OR "Character Designer") AND ("Game" OR "Animation" OR "Film") AND ("Remote" OR "Canada")
```

## 2. ⚡ Google X-Ray Searches (Direct ATS Job Pages)
Most studios post directly on Applicant Tracking Systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, SmartRecruiters) days before they show up on LinkedIn or Indeed. 

Copy and paste these exact strings into **Google Search**:

### 🟢 Greenhouse.io Jobs:
```text
site:boards.greenhouse.io ("Concept Artist" OR "2D Artist" OR "Illustrator") ("Canada" OR "Remote")
```

### 🔵 Lever.co Jobs:
```text
site:jobs.lever.co ("Concept Artist" OR "2D Artist" OR "Character Concept") ("Canada" OR "Remote")
```

### 🟣 SmartRecruiters & Ashby Jobs:
```text
(site:jobs.smartrecruiters.com OR site:jobs.ashbyhq.com) ("Concept Artist" OR "2D Artist" OR "Visual Development") ("Remote" OR "Canada")
```

### 🟠 Workday Jobs:
```text
site:myworkdayjobs.com ("Concept Artist" OR "2D Artist") ("Game" OR "Entertainment") ("Remote" OR "Canada")
```

## 3. 🤖 AI Web Search Prompt (For Perplexity, Gemini, or ChatGPT Plus)

Copy and run this weekly to find new unlisted roles:

```text
Please search the web for job openings posted within the last 14 days for the following criteria:
- Titles: Concept Artist, 2D Artist, Illustrator, Character Concept Artist, Concept Prop Artist, Visual Development Artist.
- Industries: Video Game Studios (AAA and Indie), Animation Studios, Tabletop/TCG publishers.
- Location requirements: Canada (Montreal, Vancouver, Toronto, or Canada-wide Remote) OR 100% Worldwide Remote / Global Contract.
- Experience Level: Mid-Level (also include open-level or junior/associate postings).

For each role found, output:
1. Job Title
2. Studio / Company Name
3. Location & Work Setup (Remote / Hybrid / On-site)
4. Date Posted
5. Direct Application Link
6. Quick 1-sentence note on style (e.g., Stylized, Realistic, Sci-Fi, Dark Fantasy).
```
